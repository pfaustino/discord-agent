1. What the service is

Max is a Discord bot that replies in text and voice, remembers context from your server, and carries out moderation actions when asked. It is offered two ways:

  • Managed. We host and run the bot for you, using our API keys. You buy credits, which meter what the bot spends.
  • Self-hosted. You run the software on your own infrastructure with your own keys, and we help you set it up and support it.

Max is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Discord Inc. Using it does not exempt anyone from Discord's Terms of Service or Community Guidelines, which continue to apply in full.

3. If you run a server

Adding the bot to a server means you are the one deciding what it does there, and you take on some responsibilities:

  • Tell your members. They should know the bot reads the channels it is in, transcribes voice, and stores what is said. A line in your rules channel and a link to the Privacy Policy is enough.
  • You choose the persona and the permissions. The bot adopts the persona you write and carries out moderation you authorise. What it says in your server, and what it does with the permissions you grant it, is your responsibility.
  • Handle your members' requests. If a member asks for their data to be removed, act on it — you can clear memory from the dashboard, or ask us.
  • Grant only the permissions you need. Moderation tools are owner-restricted by default, and we recommend leaving them that way.

5. AI output

Max generates replies with large language models. That means it will sometimes be confidently wrong. It can misremember, invent details, misread tone, and mis-transcribe speech — particularly in a noisy voice channel or with overlapping speakers.

Do not rely on its output for anything consequential. It is not legal, medical, financial, or professional advice, and it should not be the sole basis for a moderation decision that matters. Check anything important.

Because output is generated, identical prompts can produce different answers, and we do not guarantee any particular response. Between you and us, you own the output generated in your server, subject to the terms of the underlying model providers.

6. Credits and payment

  • Credits meter usage. One credit is one US cent of value. Replies, transcription, and speech each draw down your balance at the published rate, charged after the work is done from what it actually used — not estimated in advance.
  • Credits are issued manually. There is no payment processor connected. You arrange payment with us directly and we credit your account. We will tell you when that changes.
  • At zero, the bot stops. Metered work halts and the bot says so rather than failing silently. It resumes when credits are added. Your data is not deleted because you ran out.
  • Credits have no cash value, do not accrue interest, are not transferable between accounts, and are not redeemable for money.
  • Refunds. Unused credits are refundable at our discretion within 30 days of issue. Credits already spent are not.
  • Rates can change — provider pricing moves and we have to track it. We will give account owners notice by email before a rate change takes effect, and it will not apply retroactively.
