Privacy Policy

Max is a Discord bot that reads messages, listens in voice channels, and remembers what happened. That only works by processing what people say, so this page sets out exactly what is kept, for how long, and who else sees it.

1. Who we are

Max ("the bot", "we", "us") is operated by Travis Bollenbach. We are the controller of the data described here for bots we host on your behalf.

Two different people have a stake in any given server: the server owner, who adds the bot, configures it, and decides what it is allowed to do; and the members of that server, whose messages it processes. Where the distinction matters below, it is spelled out.

2. What we collect

From Discord servers the bot is in

  • Message content from channels the bot has been configured to read, or where it is mentioned directly. Stored with the author's Discord user ID, display name, channel, and timestamp.
  • Voice transcripts. When the bot is in a voice channel it transcribes each speaker separately. The resulting text is stored the same way message content is. See section 4 for what happens to the audio itself.
  • Derived memory. The bot periodically condenses recent conversation into running summaries, including short per-member notes about who someone is and how they talk. These are written by a language model from the conversation itself — they are not gathered from anywhere outside your server.
  • Moderation records. Warnings and moderation actions, with the target's user ID, the acting moderator's user ID, the reason given, and a timestamp.
  • Server configuration. Settings the owner chooses — persona text, which channels the bot watches, enabled features, the model it is pointed at.
  • Content deliberately given to it, such as knowledge base articles or documents a member asks it to hold onto.

From customer accounts on this website

  • Your name and email address.
  • A hashed password. We never store the password itself and cannot recover it.
  • What you told us on the order form — server name, what you want the bot to do, which tier and modules.
  • Your credit balance, credits we have issued you, and a usage record of what each bot spent them on.

What we do not collect

  • No payment details. There is no payment processor connected. Credits are issued manually by us, so no card number, bank detail, or billing address ever reaches our systems.
  • No direct messages between members. The bot cannot read them.
  • No channels it was not pointed at. The bot reads the channels the server owner configures and the ones it is mentioned in.
  • No advertising or analytics trackers on this website.

4. Voice channels

The bot joins voice channels over Discord's DAVE end-to-end encrypted protocol and transcribes each speaker separately so it can follow who said what.

Audio is never written to disk. It is held in memory only for as long as it takes to send an utterance to a transcription service and get text back, then discarded. We keep the transcript, not the recording, and we have no ability to play back anything anyone said.

Discord shows the bot in the voice channel member list like any other participant, so its presence is always visible. If you do not want to be transcribed, leave the channel or ask the server owner to disable voice.

We will also disclose data where we are legally required to, and we will tell the account owner when we are permitted to do so.

6. How long we keep it

  • Conversation turns are kept until they have been condensed into memory, and pruned after that. The bot works from a rolling recent window, not a permanent transcript archive.
  • Memory and member notes persist for as long as the bot is in your server, because that is the feature. The server owner can clear or edit them at any time from the dashboard.
  • Moderation records persist until the server owner deletes them, since their value is the history.
  • Account, order and usage records are kept while your account is open, and for a period afterwards where we need them for accounting.
  • Removing the bot from a server stops all collection immediately. Ask us and we will delete that server's stored data.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access a copy of your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, receive it in a portable form, or complain to a data protection authority. Email us and we will action it — we do not charge for this and we aim to respond within 30 days.

There are things you can do without asking us. A server member can ask the bot directly to forget what it knows about them, and can ask the server owner to have their data removed. A server owner can clear memory, delete moderation records, change which channels the bot reads, or remove the bot entirely from the dashboard.

One honest limitation: once text has been sent to a model or transcription provider, deletion on our side does not reach into their systems. Their retention is governed by their own policies.

8. Security

Traffic to this site and to the providers above is encrypted in transit. Passwords are stored as scrypt hashes with per-account salts. Dashboard access is tied to Discord roles, so moderators and server owners see different things, and customer accounts are separate from server dashboards — an account that pays for a bot is not automatically an administrator of it.

No system is perfectly secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting your data we will notify affected account owners without undue delay.

9. Age requirement

Discord requires users to be at least 13, and older in some countries. Max is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone below their local minimum age. If you believe a child's data has been collected, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Self-hosted installations

Max is also available to run yourself with your own API keys and your own database. In that case your data never touches our infrastructure, we never see it, and we are not the controller of it — you are. This policy then describes only the third-party services the software talks to, which you configure and are responsible for.

11. Changes to this policy

We will update this page when what we do changes, and move the "last updated" date at the top. For changes that meaningfully affect how your data is handled we will notify account owners by email before they take effect.
